## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory intimacy, deep heritage, and aromatic warmth of Karnataka’s homestyle and royal dining traditions. Inspired by the crackle of *oggarane* (tempering with mustard, curry leaves, and Byadgi chillies) in seasoned brass and clay cookware, the brand blends artisanal homestyle intimacy with heirloom luxury.

### Emotional Target & Aesthetic Movement
- **Heartfelt Nostalgia meets Heirloom Luxury:** The interface feels like stepping into an ancestral *thotti mane* (courtyard home) during midday festivities—rich with warmth, golden sunlight, the scent of ghee, and handcrafted brassware.
- **Design Movement:** *Tactile Heritage Minimalism*. The UI pairs clean, intentional whitespace and modern editorial typesetting with tactile grounding: brass-trimmed dividing filaments, warm ivory surfaces, hand-pressed block print motifs, and deeply saturated spice tones.
- **Audience:** Discerning food connoisseurs, diasporic food lovers craving authentic regional dishes, and culinary enthusiasts seeking authentic, handcrafted meals, curated dining experiences, and artisanal pantry provisions.

## Colors

The palette is derived from classic ingredients and kitchenware of Karnataka: sun-dried Byadgi chillies, sacred temple tulsi, aromatic banana leaves, cold-pressed oils, freshly ground turmeric, and aged clay *kadhais*.

- **Primary (`#702B25` - Deep Maroon / Byadgi Red):** Anchors primary calls to action, brand marks, and prominent culinary categories. Carries emotional resonance of auspicious kumkuma and roasted spices.
- **Secondary (`#284B38` - Forest / Banana Leaf Green):** Represents freshness, vegetarian authenticity, seasonal greens, and farm-to-table traceability. Used for secondary actions, badges, tags, and verified culinary badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D9A441` - Turmeric Gold / Heirloom Brass):** Radiates festive richness. Used for high-priority highlights, culinary rating stars, delicate hairline borders, and active selections.
- **Neutral (`#29251F` - Charcoal Slate / Cast Iron):** Replaces pure digital black with the warm carbon tone of well-seasoned cast iron, used for high-contrast, fatigue-free body typography.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFF8EB` - Warm Ivory / Payasa Cream):** Serves as the primary background foundation, avoiding cold sterile whites and providing a parchment-like warmth.
- **Accent Terracotta (`#B65C3A` - Mud Pot Red):** Applied to warm notices, seasonal chef specials, discounts, and rustic tag states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial nobility of transitional serif display fonts with the clean, modern clarity of a geometric humanist sans-serif.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Set in `Playfair Display`. Its high-contrast strokes and expressive serifs reflect classical Indian publishing and royal royal menu cards. When localized or paired with Kannada script, fallback gracefully to `Noto Serif Kannada`.
- **Interface & Nutritional Body:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Its open apertures and friendly curves maintain effortless readability across small mobile screens, order tickets, pricing tables, and ingredient lists.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** Kannada glyphs must be rendered at 105% the size of corresponding Latin characters to balance optical cap-heights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a fluid 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Margins
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins, 16px gutters. Compact, edge-to-edge culinary showcases with bottom sheets for customization.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 20px gutters. Balanced splits between recipe/dish imagery and metadata.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12 columns, max-width constrained to 1280px with minimum 64px (`4rem`) outer margins, 32px gutters. Editorial split layouts reminiscent of culinary journals.

### Rhythm
Use multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline). Dense informational blocks like spice profiles and recipe steps rely on `space-sm` and `space-md`, while editorial pauses between thali courses and chef vignettes employ `space-xl` to invoke unhurried homestyle hospitality.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of ambient culinary warmth, warm-toned diffuse glows, and brass-inspired hairlines.

- **Ambient Shading:** Shadows are tinted with Warm Terracotta and Charcoal (`rgba(41, 37, 31, 0.08)`) rather than gray. This mimics diffuse, warm golden-hour kitchen sunlight hitting surfaces.
- **Tonal Layers:** 
  - *Base:* Warm Ivory (`#FFF8EB`).
  - *Surface Elevated:* Off-white ghee tint (`#FFFDF7`) with a 1px delicate outline in brass tint (`rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.35)`).
  - *Surface Overlay:* Rich Forest Green (`#284B38`) or Deep Maroon (`#702B25`) with bright gold text for modals and sticky action banners.
- **Metallic & Hand-drawn Filaments:** Section breaks and cards feature 1px ornamental dividers incorporating geometric rangoli points or brass filigree dots rather than generic stark rules.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded aesthetic (Level 2: `0.5rem` base, `1rem` on cards, `1.5rem` on floating sheets).

- **Dish & Media Surfaces:** Dish photography cards and hero banners use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px), echoing the soft, wheel-thrown contours of clay pottery and hammered copper thali platters.
- **Chips, Pills, and Badges:** Micro-labels for dietary preferences (Pure Veg, Sattvic, Jaggery-Sweetened) use full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to differentiate functional metadata from structured layout cards.
- **Ornamental Accents:** Corner cut-outs and subtle scalloped edges inspired by traditional wooden *mantapa* pillars may be applied as SVG clip-paths on primary hero banners.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary Action (Oggarane Primary):** Deep Maroon (`#702B25`) background, Warm Ivory text, subtle bottom hairline in Turmeric Gold (`#D9A441`). Hover transitions softly to Terracotta (`#B65C3A`).
- **Secondary Action (Hasi Leaf):** Forest Green (`#284B38`) background with Warm Ivory text, used for ordering actions, cart confirmations, and dietary toggles.
- **Tertiary / Brass Outline:** Transparent surface with 1.5px solid border in Turmeric Gold (`#D9A441`), Charcoal Slate text, and Warm Ivory hover fill.

### Menu & Recipe Cards
- Built with a double layer: a Warm Ivory card base framed by a 1px subtle brass border (`rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.25)`).
- Food photography is framed at a 4:3 or 1:1 ratio with softened corners, accented by a small organic spice badge (e.g., "Mysore Style", "Ghee Roast") in the top right corner.
- Price tags are typeset in `Playfair Display` Bold, accompanied by regional currency symbols rendered with balanced optical margins.

### Dietary Chips & Taste Badges
- **Pure Veg / Banana Leaf Verified:** Forest Green pill with a mini-leaf dot icon.
- **Heritage Special / Spice Meter:** Terracotta pill with chili indicator icons.
- **Sattvic / No Onion No Garlic:** Warm ivory chip with an antique brass border.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Warm off-white (`#FFFDF7`).
- Border: 1.5px Charcoal Slate at 20% opacity (`rgba(41, 37, 31, 0.2)`).
- Focus State: 2px solid Deep Maroon (`#702B25`) border with a delicate outer halo in Turmeric Gold (`rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.25)`).
- Placeholder text in muted Charcoal Slate (`rgba(41, 37, 31, 0.5)`).

### Traditional Ornamental Dividers
- Hairline rules centered with a subtle brass diamond or mustard-seed cluster glyph, giving rhythm to long menus and multi-course thali selections.
