“ಪ್ರತಿ ತುತ್ತಿನಲ್ಲೂ ಮನೆಯ ಮಮತೆ.”

At Mane & Oggarane, cooking is never mechanized. It lives in the measured weight of a stone pestle (Oralu Kallu), the seasoned brass bowls that have witnessed three generations of weddings, and the unhurried low flame that allows mustard and ghee to fuse into memories.
